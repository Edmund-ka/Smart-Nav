## Data-flow paragraphs for the revised chapter

**Map data collection.** Public OSM records are preserved with attribution, retrieval metadata and an explicit bounding box. TSU maps are a separate visual reference whose reuse terms have not been established. All imported paths begin as candidate data.

**Route graph recording.** The internal graph represents nodes and edges with identifiers, coordinates, geometry, metres, direction and available accessibility attributes. The GeoJSON export stores nodes as Points and edges as LineStrings. Unknown attributes remain unknown. Team surveys supply verification metadata; software does not manufacture it.

**Graph loading and validation.** The loader accepts the documented schema and rejects decoding or structural errors. The validator checks references, uniqueness, geometry, length, coordinates, optional dimensions, survey evidence and connectivity. Disconnected components and isolated entrances are reported rather than joined automatically.

**Accessible path search.** Dijkstra settles the lowest-cost reachable node, relaxes outgoing permitted edges and reconstructs the chosen path. Hard restrictions remove edges before search. Preferences change only permitted costs. No-route results remain visible.

**Position and uncertainty.** Live Core Location fixes, scripted samples and saved GPS samples become timestamped `NavigationPosition` values. The session rejects stale, invalid, out-of-order and implausible observations, projects onto the route in metres and avoids choosing between indistinguishable distant route segments. Motion data remains available for later evaluation rather than being described as fused positioning.

**Guidance and output.** One session owns route progress, emitted-instruction IDs, deviation confirmation, rerouting and arrival. Structured events feed screen status, shared speech, haptics and the evaluation log. Foreground live guidance ends when its screen closes or the app becomes inactive.

**Session recording and replay.** The existing recorder retains GPS, compass, motion and manual marks. New records can retain a graph snapshot and route identity. Replay feeds every sorted original fix to the common engine using sensor time, preserving decisions at different playback speeds.

**Event comparison and trials.** Expected events are matched one-to-one within explicit timing windows. The bundled recording's manual marks are approximate annotations, not surveyed ground truth. Supervised daylight trials must separately record completion, wrong turns, assistance, recovery latency, position-reference quality, cue timing and failures. The field checklist specifies the pending accessibility review.

### Equations actually implemented

- Geographic distance: `2R asin(sqrt(sin²(Δφ/2) + cos φ₁ cos φ₂ sin²(Δλ/2)))`, with `R = 6,371,000 m`.
- Segment projection: `t = clamp(((P−A)·(B−A))/|B−A|², 0, 1)`, `Q = A+t(B−A)` in local metre coordinates; select the closest segment, subject to uncertainty/ambiguity gates. Zero-length segments are skipped. Progress is cumulative prior segment length plus `t × current length`; it is assigned only after confidence and origin checks.
- Continuity filter: displacement must be ≤ `4Δt + previousAccuracy + currentAccuracy` metres. Accuracy must be in [0,10] m; age ≤10 s; future allowance ≤1 s. Gaps >10 s reset confirmation. These are prototype parameters, not measured performance claims.
- Matching: `d+a≤8 m`; departure `max(0,d−a)≥12 m`; rejoin `d+a≤5 m`, three spaced confirmations over at least two seconds. Ambiguous projections more than 25 m apart along the route are suppressed when uncertainty cannot distinguish them.
- Cost: permitted `L × crossingFactor × roughFactor × slopeFactor`; initial applicable factors 1.2, 1.3, 1.4. Restricted/inaccessible edges are excluded; stair avoidance excludes both yes and unknown. Dijkstra relaxes `cost[v] = min(cost[v], cost[u]+weight(u,v))`.
- Turn: `atan2(cross(incoming,outgoing), dot(incoming,outgoing))`; magnitude ≥π/4 creates a turn, sign determines direction. Cue distance = instruction progress − current progress, within [0,35] m.
- Arrival: established origin, confidently matched route, remaining≤5 m and endpoint distance+accuracy≤5 m, ≥3 readings spanning≥2 s. The measured recording never meets these conditions; this is a demonstrated limitation, not validated arrival accuracy.
