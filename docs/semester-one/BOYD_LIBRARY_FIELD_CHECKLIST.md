# Boyd Hall–TSU Library field survey checklist

Use `BOYD_LIBRARY_SURVEY.svg` and the 37-edge candidate list in `CAMPUS_GRAPH_REVIEW.md`. This is a survey, not a navigation trial. Assign observer, independent reviewer, date, weather, device and source version before starting. Photograph evidence with matching waypoint/edge IDs.

- [ ] **Boyd entrance:** establish which actual door is the intended origin. Approximate anchor: 36.1654389, -86.8275056. Inspect `osm-node-6789109257` (2.56 m away, degree 1) and junction `osm-node-6789109374` (7.61 m away). Record the real door-to-path segment; do not equate a nearby node with an entrance.
- [ ] **First route section:** compare the pink traced line, green OSM path and physical sidewalk. Survey each bend and shared junction; measure lateral offsets independently of the recorded phone trace.
- [ ] **Steps after Boyd:** verify the user-reported stairs, their endpoints/direction, number of steps, handrails and a genuinely connected step-free alternative. Staged demo markers are not map evidence.
- [ ] **Departure/rejoin area:** inspect around (36.1651203, -86.8285029) and (36.1651868, -86.8285415), corresponding to old marks +103.579/+135.584 s. Identify where the intended path and actual detour were. Establish whether “left route” referred to the drawn line, sidewalk boundary or missed junction; independently measure the intended departure distance.
- [ ] **Northbound path/second turn:** inspect around (36.1657031, -86.8288021), the old second-turn fix. Record the actual decision point, turn direction, nearby parallel paths, walls and expected cue location. The old GPS accuracy there was poor.
- [ ] **Library entrance:** verify the intended door near approximate anchor 36.1658583, -86.8283694. Inspect `osm-node-6787527249` (nearest path node), `osm-node-6787527320` (degree-1 candidate) and junction `osm-node-11903003435`. Record the final approach, door threshold, clear width, hours and restrictions. The accessibility sign to the right of the door does not prove a working automatic opener; inspect and test the actual control with authorized staff as appropriate.
- [ ] **Every candidate edge:** record both endpoint IDs, shared junction identity, permitted direction, physical level, path surface, measured clear width, slope measurement method, stairs, curb cuts, crossings and restrictions. Record unknown values honestly. Do not infer accessible=yes merely from footway tagging.
- [ ] **Disconnected records:** review the corridor component list and degree-0/1 markers. There are no cross-component node pairs within 10 m in the illustrated corridor; that is not evidence that the route is physically complete. Survey entrance gaps and apparent crossings even within C1.
- [ ] **Alternative route:** survey an actual connected alternative and recovery junction for supervised rerouting. Confirm unique node association within the current 5 m uncertainty allowance is practicable. Otherwise record no-route behavior as the expected result.
- [ ] **Independent reference:** use identifiable fixed landmarks and measured offsets, with observer timestamps synchronized to the phone. A second walker independently checks geometry and attributes. Record method/estimated uncertainty; do not claim survey-grade GPS from a phone.
- [ ] **Fresh measurements:** collect stationary origin acquisition before moving, repeat normal walks in both directions, label each turn explicitly, document marking delays, and dwell at the actual destination. Preserve raw fixes including bad readings. Capture repeated conditions rather than fitting one trace.
- [ ] **Release review:** add only evidenced edges/entrance associations; include checker/date/source, graph version and supporting photos. Revalidate both JSON and GeoJSON, check route direction/restrictions and independent review. Mark verified only after the evidence is approved. Preserve old data for reproducibility.
